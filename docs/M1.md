# Milestone 1: engine, content and simulator

**Goal:** the battle rules run headless in a typed, deterministic engine (`src/engine/`) that reads its numbers from `src/content/`. A simulator then shows the engine plays like 3.x.

**Done when:** simulated win rates and end-of-level castle health, for every built level on each difficulty, match a baseline recorded from 3.x. "Match" means within the spread the old game shows between its own runs.

The live game (`src/main.js`) keeps running unchanged throughout. Nothing here is bundled until milestone 2 wires the renderer to the engine.

## Rules for this milestone

- **The engine is pure.** No DOM, Three.js, clock or `Math.random`: randomness comes from `src/engine/rng.ts`, carried in the state.
- **No retyped numbers.** Numbers come from `src/content/`, which `npm run extract` copies from `main.js`. `test/content.test.ts` fails if the two drift. Until 3.5 ships, balance changes go in `main.js` first, then you run `npm run extract`.
- **Port first, improve later.** Port behaviour as it is, including quirks. Note any bug found along the way here; fix it in `main.js` and the engine together.
- **Tests:** every ported system gets a Vitest file in `test/` that fails when its rule breaks.

## Steps

- [x] **Tooling:** TypeScript, Vitest and tsx; the `test`, `typecheck` and `extract` scripts.
- [x] **Content:** maps, enemies, towers, barracks and drum, spells, campaign, skill tree and AI weights, extracted with a parity test. `RANKX` (tooltip text) stays with the UI.
- [x] **Rng and waves:** seeded mulberry32; `hpMul`, `waveCounts` and `waveGroups`.
- [x] **Map geometry** (`src/engine/map.ts`): tile grid, paths, `pathAt` and `smoothPath`, AI samples, build plots and the castle centre.
    - Plot placement uses the fixed `fbm` noise, not `Math.random`, so it was already deterministic.
    - `test/map.test.ts` matches all 7 maps bit for bit against the 3.x game: plots, path lengths, samples and tiles.
    - The gate and the flag are only drawn, so they stay with the renderer.
- [x] **Enemies** (`src/engine/enemies.ts`, game state in `src/engine/state.ts`): spawning, walking the road with the spread across it, the bog, slows, freezes, stuns and burns, armour and magic resist, kills and rewards, the castle hit and Last Stand. Specials: Shaman heals, Sapper charges, the Boar Rider's shrug, Gruk's roar and slow resistance, and flyers. Tested in `test/enemies.test.ts`.
    - Centre heights come from the 3D models, recorded with `window.__enemyDims()` into `src/content/enemy-dims.ts`. Ranges measured centre to centre in 3D (Shaman heal, Static Charge, Shatter, Wildfire) depend on them.
    - Melee is a placeholder (`src/engine/combat.ts`) until the Barracks step ports `enemyCombat`; the Boar Rider's charge into a soldier lives there.
    - The mark countdown, Bleed and Frost Bite tick in a War Council block in 3.x, so they move with that step.
- [x] **Towers** (`src/engine/towers.ts`, `src/engine/projectiles.ts`):
    - stats by level, specialisation and rank, with the War Council's stat picks; costs and sell refunds; the unlock gate; building, upgrading, specialising and selling, with plot blocking;
    - targeting modes and the mortar's dead zone;
    - firing for all five attacking towers and their War Council abilities;
    - arrows, cannonballs and bomblets, frost shards, Tesla chains and Overload, flame cones, fire and ice patches.
    - `test/towers.test.ts` matches every stat, cost and sell rate in 3.x exactly (`window.__towerStats`), with no picks and with every stat pick. Behaviour tests cover the rest.
    - **Approximation:** projectiles start straight above the tower, at the muzzle height recorded with `window.__muzzles()`. In 3.x they start at the turning head's muzzle, up to about .3 to one side. That changes an arrow's flight by about .02 s; the simulator will show whether it matters.
- [x] **Barracks** (`src/engine/soldiers.ts`, `src/engine/combat.ts`):
    - soldier stats with the War Council picks;
    - spawning at the rally point, respawn, regen, rally placement;
    - targeting, blocking up to 2 enemies, the fan-out with the 3.1 fix;
    - sword strikes, shield bashes, Paladins' holy strikes and their War Council abilities;
    - Rangers and Overwatch;
    - enemy attacks (`ATK`), with the Boar Rider's charge, the Ogre's cleave, bosses' splash and the Shaman's ranged bolt.
    - Every soldier stat matches 3.x exactly (`window.__barrStats`). `test/barracks.test.ts` covers the rest, including a check that fails with the old spin bug: 14 radians of turning in 3 s.
- [x] **War Drum** (`src/engine/drum.ts`): stats with the War Council picks, the boost pass (haste, the War Horn's range, Twin Drums, Last Stand's haste), the Hex Totem's hex, Thunder and Charge. Drum stats match 3.x exactly (`window.__drumStats`); behaviour in `test/drum.test.ts`.
    - `drumPass` also runs the War Council's timed effects (Last Stand's countdown, Arrow Slits, the Hunter's Mark, Bleed, Frost Bite), as 3.x does, so those are done too.
- [x] **Spells** (`src/engine/spells.ts`): which spells each level opens, the first-wave lock, cooldowns, aiming (Militia snap to the road), Militia Call (two militiamen for 12 s, using the ported soldiers), Catapult Barrage (five boulders from the castle, landing at about 3 s) and Frost Nova. Tested in `test/spells.test.ts`.
    - `game.spellUsed` is set in 3.x but never read. The engine keeps it in case a star rule wants it.
- [x] **Economy and rules** (`src/engine/game.ts`, `makeGame` in `src/engine/state.ts`):
    - starting gold and castle health, with the Kingdom picks;
    - difficulty by name;
    - Watch mode's random toughness;
    - kill rewards, the early-call bonus and the wave-clear bonus (Muster, Interest, Repair);
    - victory, loss and stars.
    - Every War Council pick is now ported with its system.
- [x] **Game loop** (`tick(g, inputs)` in `src/engine/game.ts`): a fixed 30 Hz step in `updateGame` order. The inputs are build, upgrade, specialise, sell, aim, rally, call wave and cast. Speed and pause are the renderer's job: it runs more ticks, or none.
    - `test/game.test.ts` plays whole battles: Millbrook won by a simple player, a battle with no towers lost, and the same seed and inputs replaying exactly.
- [x] **AI player** (`src/engine/ai.ts`):
    - `aiThink` is Watch mode's bot. Every .7 s it scores builds and upgrades as road covered × damage × need ÷ cost, where need follows the `WANT` mix and the next wave's armour, magic resist and flyers.
    - `makeWatchGame` sets up Watch mode's opening: one of each tower, little gold, wave 1 already on the road, 3 s played.
    - Tested in `test/ai.test.ts`. A first run of 5 seeds per map: Millbrook 5/5 won, River 4/5, Thornwood 5/5, Mire 4/5, Highland Pass 3/5, Frostmere 1/5, Ember Crater 2/5. About .6 s per battle.
    - **Approximation:** 3.x shuffles the opening towers with a biased `sort(() => Math.random() - .5)`; the engine uses a fair shuffle.
- [x] **Baseline from 3.x** (`test/baseline.json`): 40 Watch battles per map, played in the live game with `window.__watchRun(i)`. Each run records the win, castle health, wave reached, battle length, toughness and towers built.
- [x] **Simulator** (`npm run sim`, `tools/sim.ts`): plays the same Watch battles on the engine and compares each map's mean win rate, castle health, wave reached and win length with the baseline. A measure passes when the gap is under 2.5 standard errors of the difference, using both samples' own spread.

## Result (2026-11-24)

**Milestone 1 is done.** With 80 engine runs per map against 40 from 3.x, every map matches on all four measures:

| Map | Wins 3.x / engine | Castle hp 3.x / engine | Win length (s) 3.x / engine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Millbrook Fields | 100% / 100% | 99 / 99 | 160 / 155 |
| River Crossing | 82% / 82% | 64 / 69 | 636 / 628 |
| Thornwood | 100% / 100% | 96 / 96 | 482 / 514 |
| Mire's Edge | 78% / 72% | 40 / 40 | 863 / 852 |
| Highland Pass | 57% / 64% | 39 / 37 | 918 / 897 |
| Frostmere | 30% / 44% | 12 / 20 | 882 / 881 |
| Ember Crater | 42% / 46% | 20 / 18 | 1241 / 1241 |

Frostmere leans slightly easier on the engine but stays inside the limit. If a later change pushes it further, start with the approximations noted above: projectiles starting above the tower, and the fair opening shuffle.

## Found while porting

- **Dead tower abilities in 3.x:** `pierce`, `flak`, `pin`, `delay` (charges), `shrap` (shrapnel), `resid` and `debt` are read when a tower fires, but nothing ever switches them on. The engine leaves them out. Delete them from `main.js` when the renderer moves over, or give them War Council picks.
- **Shamans could stall a battle forever (3.x bug, fixed 2026-11-24 in 3.1 and the engine).** A Shaman stopped walking while it shot a soldier 3 tiles away. Three Shamans just outside every tower's reach shot a Barracks' soldiers and healed each other 8% every 2 s, while the soldiers kept respawning; after 1,100 s nothing had changed. About 1 in 40 Watch battles on Mire's Edge, Frostmere and Ember Crater hit the 1,500 s cap this way. Now Shamans keep walking while they shoot and stop only when a soldier blocks them (`enemyCombat` returns `!e.shooting`). `test/baseline.json` was recorded before the fix.
- **Shard `brittle`** (doubles damage on slowed enemies via `o.brittle = 3`) is never set to true in 3.x either.

- The soldier fan-out spin (fixed in 3.1, commit `58a9d90`): port the fixed version.
