# Milestone 2: the renderer runs on the engine

Goal: battles run on `src/engine/` (milestone 1), and `src/main.js` only draws them and sends the player's actions. One copy of the rules, tested and deterministic. The 3D models, animation, effects, HUD, menus, Almanac and cloud saves stay as they are.

Work happened on the `m2/renderer-port` branch, with the engine behind a flag until the switch-over. **Milestone 2 is done:** the engine runs every battle and `main.js` has no battle rules left (about 850 lines of 3.x rules deleted).

## How it fits together

- **One engine game per battle.** `resetGame` makes `G = makeGame(...)` with the map, mode, seed, difficulty, War Council picks, campaign gate and spells. Watch mode also gets the engine's AI player and opening (`makeWatchGame`).
- **Fixed steps.** `updateGame(dt)` collects the frame's inputs and runs `tick(G, inputs)` at 30 steps a second (speed 2× and 3× run more steps). Each step's events go to the renderer.
- **Views mirror engine objects.** The existing `Enemy`, `Tower` and `Soldier` classes become views: each keeps its models and animation and reads its engine record (`src`) for position, heading, health and status timers. Views are created on `spawn` and `build` events (and soldiers when they appear) and removed when the record dies.
- **The UI reads `game` as now.** After each frame, `game`'s battle fields (gold, castle health, wave, state, queue, countdown, cooldowns) are copied from `G`, so the HUD, tooltips, director and tutorial keep working unchanged.
- **The player's actions become inputs.** Building, upgrading, specialising, selling, aiming, moving a rally point, calling a wave and casting a spell push an `Input` instead of changing the game directly.
- **Events become effects.** `kill` gives debris and coins, `gate` damages the castle, `fire` plays the tower's shot, `blast` an explosion, `roar` and `stomp` their shakes and messages, `victory` and `castleFalls` the endings, and so on.
- **Projectiles are drawn from engine records** (start, aim, progress and arc are kept for this), so shots land exactly when the engine says.

## Notes

- **Checked: the engine runs a little easier than 3.2 on Highland Pass and Ember Crater.** Against the new baseline (160 engine runs): Highland 71% wins against 53%, Ember 51% against 33% (castle health 22 against 10, just outside the limit). Every rule change since milestone 1 (Shamans shooting on the move, the Mire Stomp) was diffed line by line between `main.js` and the engine, and so were every read of `fighting` and `shooting`: they match. 3.x's own two recordings swing as much with no relevant change (Frostmere 30% → 45%, Ember 42% → 33%), so this is within what 40 runs per map can resolve. If it matters, record more 3.x runs from a 3.2 build (the `?legacy` path is gone after the switch-over).

- Shots that the engine resolves at once (Tesla chains, Meltdown, Flashover) carry what the renderer needs in their events (`fire.chains`, `surge`, `flashover`). They don't change the rules.
- Still drawn by 3.x code only: the War Drum's Thunder and Charge pulses (no engine event yet), and a Shaman's per-target heal sparkles (the engine's `heal` event has only a count).
