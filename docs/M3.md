## First-pass balance

Aldric Vane, the Last Captain, holds three enemies and reduces damage to soldiers within three tiles. Rally the Line plants a six-second banner. Shield Wall protects Aldric for four seconds. The Gate Holds charges through hits taken, then places a spectral gate across his current lane for eight seconds.

Corwin Ashguard, the Ember Warden, fights at range. Lantern Light weakens magic resistance and prevents Shaman healing within three tiles. Kindle sets a five-second fire patch on the road. Ashen Ward protects a tower and ignites targets hit by it. Undying Coal can be used once per battle to burst burning enemies for magic damage and award bonus gold for burning kills from then on.

Both heroes are available in the battle picker. Recruitment and progression are outside this milestone.

Tap the hero portrait and then walkable ground to move. Kindle targets a road point. Ashen Ward targets a tower. The other abilities cast from the hero's current position.
